About Us

Our Story

The Lae Handicraft Market was founded to celebrate Papua New Guinea’s rich cultural heritage. Local artisans bring their skills and traditions to life through handmade crafts, clothing, and artworks that reflect the diversity of PNG’s communities.

Our Mission

We aim to support local craftspeople by providing a platform to showcase and sell their creations. Every purchase helps sustain traditional practices and empowers families across the region.

Why Choose Us

By visiting the Lae Handicraft Market, you experience authentic PNG culture firsthand. From bilums to meri blouses, each item tells a story of identity, resilience, and artistry.
